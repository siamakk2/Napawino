Two downtown rooms carry Napa's touring concert calendar, and dozens of wineries run harvest dinners you won't find on a wine-tasting map. This page tracks both — who's actually playing, who's cooking what, and where.

Napa Music Hall — 1030 Main Street

The historic Napa Valley Opera House, reimagined into two rooms under new management in 2026: The Ballroom upstairs, about 650 capacity, for standing-room touring acts, and The Club below, roughly 160 seated, for jazz, blues, and acoustic sets. Both walkable from Oxbow Public Market.

Sat 14 Nov · Napa Music Hall

The California Honeydrops

Oakland-based soul, blues and funk band fronted by Lech Wierzynski, known for a horn-driven, New Orleans-tinged live sound.

Sun 22 Nov · Uptown Theatre

The Residents, with Rushadicus

Anonymous, mask-wearing San Francisco avant-garde art-rock group active since 1972, touring their "Eskimo Live!" show.

Full calendars at napamusichall.com and uptowntheatrenapa.com/events.

Harvest dinners worth booking

The fall harvest-dinner circuit has mostly wrapped for the year — see the full harvest guide for how the vintage went. A few late-season pairing dinners are still open for booking.

Wed 28 Oct · Auro, St. Helena

Hundred Acre Wine Dinner

Finale of Auro's 2026 Wine Dinner Series: a six-course tasting menu paired with Hundred Acre's limited-production, single-vineyard Cabernet Sauvignons, plus selections from sister labels Fortunate Son and Summer Dreams. 21+.$850/person

More harvest dinners are added throughout the season — the fullest running list is kept by Napa Valley Vintners. Most require advance reservations and sell out once announced.
