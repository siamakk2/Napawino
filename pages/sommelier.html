What it sounds like

Three real prompts, and the kind of answer you'll get

“I like bold Cabernets and want a view.”

Expect estates on Pritchard Hill, Howell Mountain or Diamond Mountain District, where elevation gives both the structure in the glass and the sightline — ordered so you're not driving the mountain roads twice in one afternoon.

"First trip — plan me 3 stops with a lunch between."

A beginner-friendly route: one approachable, well-known estate to start, one slightly further afield, a lunch table slotted between stops two and three, and a third stop that doesn't require backtracking to reach.

"A day in Napa with no driving."

Walkable picks concentrated in one town — typically downtown Napa or Yountville — so the whole day works on foot or with one short rideshare, with a restaurant in the same few blocks.

Questions about the sommelier

Napa Sommelier FAQ

What is the Napa sommelier?

The Napa sommelier, called The Suggester, is a free AI tool on Napa Wino that recommends Napa Valley wineries and builds a tasting route based on what you describe. It draws from a database of 50+ verified estates across all 16 Napa Valley AVAs and never accepts paid placements.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free, requires no account or sign-up, and there's no limit on how many questions you can ask.

How does it pick wineries?

It matches your description — grape preferences, mood, group size, whether you want a view, caves, or something kid- or dog-friendly — against tags on each winery, then orders the picks geographically so your day follows one direction along Highway 29 or the Silverado Trail instead of backtracking.

Can it plan a full day, not just one winery?

Yes. Ask it for a full day and it typically returns two to three wineries in a sensible order plus a restaurant suggestion placed between stops — the standard Napa pacing of two to three tastings per day.

Does it book reservations?

No. It recommends wineries and routes but doesn't book tastings or restaurants. Most Napa wineries are appointment-only, so book directly once you have your plan — a week ahead off-season, months ahead for harvest season or icon estates.

Does the sommelier get paid to recommend certain wineries?

No. Napa Wino's wine guide, including the sommelier's recommendations, accepts zero paid placements. Real-estate listings elsewhere on the site are funded separately and never influence which wineries the sommelier suggests.
